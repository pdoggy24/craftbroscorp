About Craft Bros Corp

Craft Bros Corp offers 3D CAD design and 3D printing.

This site is intentionally simple — no product cart, no online checkout. You describe the project; you get a follow-up by email with a quote.

I design and 3D print custom parts tailored to each client's needs — including replacement and replica components. Whether you need adapters, fittings, or another specialized part, I model the solution in CAD and deliver it ready for production.
